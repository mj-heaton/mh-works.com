Numeri

Italian numbers at the speed people say them · iPhone and Android

Numeri drills the part of Italian that stays hard long after the grammar settles: catching a number, a time or a price at the speed someone says it, and producing one back without stopping to work it out.

Numeri is not released yet. When it is on the App Store and Google Play, the links will be on this page. Until then there is deliberately nothing here pretending otherwise: no screenshots, no reviews, and no download buttons that lead nowhere.

What you practise

  • Numbers, heard and said rather than read off a page.
  • Clock times, the way they are actually spoken, from l'una to le ventitré, with the halves and the quarters.
  • Prices in euro.
  • Percentages.
  • Temperatures, including below zero.
  • Ordinals: first, second, third and onwards.

How it behaves on your device

  • No account and no sign-in. Install it and play.
  • Works offline. Every question and every recording is built into the app, so the game itself never needs a connection. Nothing to download, and no server of ours that can be down.
  • No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifiers and no analytics watching how you play.
  • One purchase, not a subscription. The download is free, and a single in-app purchase unlocks the full content.

Your progress and your settings are held on your device and nowhere else. That cuts both ways: they are nobody else's business, and they stay on that phone. A new phone starts you fresh unless your own device backup happens to carry the app's data across.

What it costs

Numeri is free to download. One in-app purchase, £2.99 in the UK and priced by the store in your own currency elsewhere, unlocks the full content permanently. It is a one-off purchase rather than a subscription, and there is nothing further to buy.

Apple and Google take the payment and are the merchant of record, so the store shows you the exact price before you confirm anything and MH Works never sees your card details. The support page covers restoring a purchase and how refunds work.

Privacy, in one paragraph

Numeri has no accounts and collects no personal data. It carries no ads, no tracking and no advertising identifiers, and nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone, because there is nothing to sell. The only thing the app itself ever sends over a network is a crash report when it breaks, so the fault can be found and fixed. Those reports carry no identity and no IP address, and you can switch them off in the app's settings. The Numeri privacy policy sets all of that out in full, including what a crash report does and does not contain.
