Games

Two Italian practice games for iPhone and Android

MH Works Ltd publishes two standalone Italian practice games. Each does one narrow thing, both run entirely on your own device, and neither asks you to make an account.

Neither game has been released yet, so there are no store links to give you. These pages exist because the store listings, the support contact and the privacy policies need a permanent home before the games go up.

  • Numeri

    Hearing and saying Italian numbers, times, prices and percentages at speed.

    About Numeri Support

  • Coniuga!

    Italian verb conjugation, 992 verbs in frequency order, every form voiced.

    About Coniuga! Support

What both games have in common

  • No account and no sign-in. Install it and play.
  • Works offline. Every question and every recording is built into the app, so the game itself never needs a connection. Nothing to download, and no server of ours that can be down.
  • No ads, no tracking, no advertising identifiers and no analytics watching how you play.
  • One purchase, not a subscription. The download is free, and a single in-app purchase unlocks the full content.
